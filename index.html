The two-hour curve

You leave. The clock starts.

For many dogs, the first two hours after the door clicks shut are the hardest. The house goes quiet, the routine stops, and all that energy has nowhere to go. PawClock gives it somewhere to land.

  1. 0:00

    The door clicks

    The house goes quiet. For many dogs, that is the cue for a stress spike.

  2. 0:20

    Restlessness

    Pacing, whining, watching the window — the energy has nowhere to go.

  3. 1:00

    Destructive relief

    Chewing, digging and barking are often just displacement behaviours.

  4. 2:00

    The settle

    Most dogs finally calm down — usually after the hardest part is over.

PawClock fills that gap with a guided sequence of natural calming behaviours: licking, scent work, chewing and rest.

The ritual

Four phases, one quiet countdown.

Each phase meets a different need, in the order a dog naturally reaches for them. Stack them for a two-hour sequence, or use one at a time.

Lick

20–30 min

Frozen lick-well bowl

Slow, rhythmic licking is a natural self-soother. Fill the well, freeze it overnight, and clip it on for the first stretch after you leave. The frozen lick-well bowl is built for calm, repeatable licking.

  • Food-grade silicone
  • Freezer-ready
  • Dishwasher-safe

Sniff

15–20 min

Maze ball food dispenser

A treat-first toy. Treats hide inside a rolling maze ball, turning restless energy into focused foraging instead of pacing. The ball works as both a dog toy and a food dispenser, keeping the scent trail moving and engaging without getting stuck under furniture.

  • Treat chamber
  • Adjustable difficulty
  • Quiet on floors
  • Dog toy + food dispenser

Chew

20–30 min

Dental-chew core

A textured ring redirects stress into a healthy chew, massaging gums while the treat reward lasts. Swap the core as your dog's chewing style changes.

  • Vet-friendly texture
  • Replaceable core
  • Treat-stuffable

Settle

60 min

Weighted chrono base

A 1.4 kg base keeps every module steady while a mechanical timer quietly counts down the last stretch of alone time. No app. No alerts. Just a calm, familiar rhythm. The settle phase can be paired with a calming scent routine chosen around your dog’s favourite flavours and tolerance.

  • Mechanical timer
  • No batteries
  • Non-slip base

Phase 04 is a calm-down finish, not just a timer. Use a soothing scent routine that matches your dog’s favourite flavours, and read the calming-scent guide in detail before testing new aromas.

Questions

A few honest answers.

Is PawClock safe to leave unsupervised?

Every module is pet-safe and chew-resistant, but no enrichment device replaces your vet's advice. Start with short sessions, inspect the modules regularly, and retire the chew core at the first sign of deep wear.

Does it need an app or batteries?

No. The chrono base is fully mechanical. Twist to set the sequence and PawClock runs the two-hour cycle without a phone, a battery or a subscription.

How do I clean it?

The lick-well, scent labyrinth and chew core are top-rack dishwasher-safe. Wipe the brass base with a damp cloth and mild soap; do not submerge it.

What size dogs is it for?

PawClock is designed for dogs from 5 kg upward. The weighted base suits most homes; for determined power chewers, choose the reinforced chew core when it launches.

PawClock is a concept study. Reservations are free and refundable, and we will confirm timing and final specifications before any payment is taken.
